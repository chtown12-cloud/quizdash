How to write a quiz 
# Friday Quiz

## What is the capital of France?
- Paris *
- London
- Berlin
  • # the title
  • ## a question
  • - an answer
  • * marks the right one

That's a whole quiz. For other kinds of question, change what goes under the ##:

no * at allPoll: no points, up to 10 options
several *Pick all that apply
= 206Closest number wins
= 275-300Closest number with a range. Anything in between is spot on
= MarsType the answer. Add more = lines for other spellings
nothingWord cloud: everyone types a word
paste a pictureShows with the question. Click aim in the green box to make it tap the spot

The green box under your quiz shows how each question was read.

Timers and double points
  • # Friday Quiz | 30s | read 5s sets the seconds to answer, and to read first, for every question (normally 20 and 5)
  • ## A tricky one? 45s changes just this question
  • ## The big finale? x2 is worth double points
  • A picture from the web: paste its link on its own line
  • A picture that only appears with the answer: ![answer](link), or paste one and write answer in its brackets
  • Right answers score 500 to 1000. The faster, the more
